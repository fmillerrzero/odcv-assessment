O-DCV Assessment Form

This form assesses your building's readiness for Occupancy-Driven Demand-Controlled Ventilation (O-DCV), a system that optimizes ventilation rates based on real-time occupancy data. O-DCV can reduce energy costs by 10-30% while maintaining excellent indoor air quality in commercial buildings.

Privacy Policy

Data Collection and Usage

The information collected in this O-DCV Assessment Form is used solely for the purpose of evaluating your building's suitability for Occupancy-Driven Demand-Controlled Ventilation implementation. We collect this data to:

  • Assess technical feasibility based on your current building systems
  • Identify potential energy savings opportunities
  • Determine appropriate pilot areas
  • Create a baseline for measuring ROI

Data Handling

All information submitted is secured using industry-standard encryption during transmission and storage. Your data is:

  • Stored in secure, access-controlled systems
  • Only accessible to authorized personnel involved in your assessment
  • Never sold to third parties
  • Only shared with implementation partners with your explicit permission

File Uploads

Photos and documents uploaded via this form are stored securely. By uploading files, you:

  • Confirm you have the right to share these documents
  • Grant us permission to review them for assessment purposes only
  • Understand uploaded files are subject to the same privacy protections as form data

Data Retention

We retain your assessment data for a period of 3 years after submission or until project completion, whichever is longer. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting our privacy team.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we have collected about you
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for further data processing
